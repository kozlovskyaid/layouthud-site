First Launch & the Accessibility Permission

  1. Open LayoutHUD. It lives in the menu bar (the ⌨️ icon) — there is no Dock icon, the app stays out of your way.
  2. Grant the Accessibility permission. macOS will ask for it on first launch: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → enable LayoutHUD. The app needs it to detect which text field you are typing in and where it is on screen — that is the only thing the permission is used for.
  3. Click into any text field. The badge appears next to it and disappears when you leave the field. Tune size, fonts, colors and position from the menu bar icon → Settings.

You can revoke the permission at any time in System Settings — the badge will simply stop appearing until access is granted again. Details in the Privacy Policy.

LayoutHUD works entirely on your Mac: settings live on your device, the app works offline, and the Accessibility permission is used only to place the indicator next to the field where you type.
