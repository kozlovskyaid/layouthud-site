Privacy Policy

LayoutHUD for macOS

LayoutHUD works entirely on your Mac. Your settings live on your device and stay under your control.

Data the App Uses (on your Mac only)

LayoutHUD works with the minimum information necessary to function:

  • Appearance settings: size, fonts, colors and position of the indicator — stored locally on your Mac.
  • Keyboard focus information: the app uses the macOS Accessibility permission solely to determine which interface element currently has keyboard focus and where it is on screen, so the layout indicator can be positioned next to it. This information is processed on your Mac in real time and is not stored.
  • Current keyboard layout: the app reads the identifier and name of the active input source in order to display it.

Data Storage

  • All settings are stored locally on your Mac
  • The app works offline; no account is needed
  • You can delete all app data at any time by removing the app

Data Sharing

As of the date of this policy:

  • The app does not transmit your data to the developer or to third parties
  • The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs

Accessibility Permission

LayoutHUD requests the macOS Accessibility permission (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility). It is required to detect the focused text field and its position on screen. You can revoke this permission at any time; the indicator will stop appearing until access is granted again.

Your Rights

  • Change or reset all settings directly in the app
  • Revoke the Accessibility permission in System Settings at any time
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its data

Children's Privacy

LayoutHUD does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
